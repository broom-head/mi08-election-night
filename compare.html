MI-08 sheet check

Compares two copies of the election-night tracker cell by cell: every candidate's E-Day (Tapes), AV and Early cells and the third-party cells, on the tracker tab and the State Leg Races tab, row by row. Read only; nothing is written to either sheet.
Both links are remembered in this browser. The check runs through the poller, which reads both sheets as its own account (it needs view access to each). A row is matched by County, Muni and Precinct.
 

Cells that differ

Each line names the precinct, then exactly where the cell sits in each sheet (tab, row, cell address). A − B treats a blank as 0.
